# MLIFT
Abstract: Hydraulic pump systems are usually arranged in multiple sensors to monitor the condition of the equipment. The conventional fault diagnosis methods relying on single-source signals can no longer meet the requirements of hydraulic pump operation guarantee. Therefore, the fault diagnosis method based on the fusion of multi-source information has received extensive attention. However, the current fusion methods fail to effectively take into account the local details and global trend features of multi-source data in feature extraction. The lack of consideration of the intrinsic correlation and distribution differences between features at different scales leads to the reduced generalization performance of the model. In order to solve the above problems and improve the accuracy and generalization ability of hydraulic pump fault diagnosis. In this paper, a multi-scale linear interaction fusion transformer (MLIFT) method is proposed. First, the local and global feature extraction capability of multi-source data is enhanced by joint multi-scale temporal convolution and channel convolution. Second, the Pearson correlation coefficient is used as the attention weighting index. Combined with the multi-scale linear interaction transformer to enhance the synergistic characterization ability between the features of multi-source data. Finally, the fusion classification module based on deep learning is constructed to realize hydraulic pump fault pattern recognition. The test cases are validated using fault data from two different models of hydraulic pumps. The experimental results show that the accuracy and noise robustness of MLIFT are better than other comparative fusion methods. The minimum accuracy on different datasets reaches 97.92%. Even in a strong noise environment with a signal-to-noise ratio of -10db, the indexes can still be maintained above 90%.
